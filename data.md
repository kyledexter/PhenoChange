<section class="page-hero"><div class="shell narrow"><p class="eyebrow">Data</p><h1>Shared observations, transparent methods and responsible access.</h1><p class="lead">PhenoChange is building a coordinated record of canopy dynamics across tropical dry forests and savannas.</p></div></section>

<section class="section"><div class="shell data-grid"><article><span class="feature-number">01</span><h2>Camera observations</h2><p>High-frequency repeat photographs document canopy condition and seasonal transitions at each monitoring site.</p></article><article><span class="feature-number">02</span><h2>Derived time series</h2><p>Image-based colour indices can quantify greenness and support estimation of growing-season transitions.</p></article><article><span class="feature-number">03</span><h2>Site metadata</h2><p>Environmental context, camera configuration, vegetation type and sampling history make comparisons robust.</p></article></div></section>
