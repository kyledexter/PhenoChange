      <p class="eyebrow">Why phenology matters</p>
      <h2>Seasonal leaf dynamics regulate entire ecosystems.</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="prose-large">
      <p>The timing of canopy greening and browning shapes carbon uptake, water use, energy exchange and habitat conditions. Yet dry tropical ecosystems remain underrepresented in long-term phenological monitoring.</p>
      <p>By connecting researchers and camera sites across tropical dry forests and savannas, PhenoChange will create comparable, high-frequency observations across regions and continents.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>
